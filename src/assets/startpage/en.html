KantSearch - A Search Application for the Bonn Kant Corpus

Usage guide

  • The original pagination of the Academy Edition is shown in square brackets in the text.
  • Searches are paragraph-based rather than page-based. This means that searching for a combination of terms or phrases does not look for which page the combination or phrase appears on, but rather in which paragraph. As a result, matches are also found where a paragraph spans multiple pages.
  • Instructions on combining search terms and the spelling of search terms can be found in the "Search Tips" section on the search page.
  • All search parameters (search terms, work selection, and advanced options) are encoded in the URL. You can save your search settings by bookmarking the search results page. You can find a button to copy the current URL to the clipboard next to the search term input field on the results page.
  • Page and line numbers of search hits on the results page are shown separated by a colon and enclosed in square brackets.
  • The loading performance of texts and search results is not yet optimized. Please have patience if it takes a moment for a page to load, especially when a search returns a lot of results.

Spelling Heuristics for Search Terms (selection)

  • Latin or latinized loanwords containing 'k' or 'z' are generally spelled with 'c'.
  • Endings '-ier' (as in 'existieren') are often spelled '-ir' ('existiren').
  • Some native German words are spelled with 'th' instead of 't'. These include, among others, 'Teil', 'Mut', 'tun', 'Tier', and words ending in '-tum'.
  • 'gibt' and derived forms ('ergibt', 'abgibt', ...) are spelled 'giebt'.
  • endings '-nis' are often spelled '-niß' (e.g. 'Ereigniß' instead of 'Ereignis').
  • .'ss' following a vowel within a syllable is usually spelled 'ß' (e.g. 'Fluß' instead of 'Fluss').
  • 'Rasse' and derived words such as 'Menschenrasse' are often spelled 'Race'.
  • 'transzendental' and its inflected forms are spelled with 'c' and a double 's' ('transscendental').

Differences from the Print Academy Edition

  • All Roman numerals are displayed as Arabic numerals in the website text.
  • In "Review of Silberschlag's Work: Theory of the Fireball that Appeared on July 23, 1762" from Volume II, the page numbers in the print edition are given as page 272 with letter suffixes (272a, 272b, etc.). Here, they are always indicated as "272," so this page number appears multiple times in the text.
  • Volume I contains eight paragraph summaries that are not placed at the beginning but in the middle of a paragraph. These eight paragraph summaries are not included in the text here.
  • In the Critique of Judgment, two subheadings "Einleitung" were added: after "Zweites Buch. Analytik des Erhabenen." and after "Zweiter Theil. Kritik der teleologischen Urtheilskraft."

Feedback and Contribution

Please contact us if you have feedback for us or find errors in the texts. For technical issues or suggestions for improving the website, feel free to send an email to kant-search@mailbox.org in German or English, or create an issue in the Github repository linked below. For issues related to the texts themselves, you can also contact one of the Bonn Kant Corpus representatives directly.

The website's code is open source and can be viewed in the project's Github repository. If you want to contribute the the development of the application, please contact us at the email address provided above or submit a pull request in the Github repositories.
